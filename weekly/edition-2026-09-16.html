NHTSA Special Order on Cybercab FMVSS certification (AQ26002)

Thursday, September 10, 2026 · Legal · Tesla

Dated September 10, 2026, NHTSA issued a Special Order in AQ26002 requiring Tesla to provide sworn answers by September 30, 2026 on the process and technical data behind Cybercab’s FMVSS self-certification after commercial service began September 3. The order restates that manufacturers may not issue a certification if, exercising reasonable care, they have reason to know it is false or misleading. Local archive copy saved under filings/.

announced pending — Deadline moved to October 30, 2026. A NHTSA memo to the AQ26002 file dated September 29, 2026 says Tesla asked on September 18 for eight more weeks (to November 20); on September 22 NHTSA granted 30 days instead, making Tesla's sworn response due October 30, 2026. The memo was added to the public file around October 7 and first reported October 8. No finding against Cybercab and no public resolution yet. As of 2026-10-15 daily check, no newer public NHTSA memo or Tesla response filing; sworn answers still due October 30, 2026. As of 2026-10-26 daily check, no public Tesla response or new NHTSA document in AQ26002; sworn answers still due October 30, 2026. As of 2026-10-30 morning PT daily check, no public Tesla response or new NHTSA document in AQ26002; sworn answers due today, October 30, 2026. Tesla met the October 30 deadline with a response marked entirely confidential; Question 8 certification answers are not on the public docket (Electrek Nov 5; TeslaNorth Nov 3). Confidentiality ruling and audit outcome still pending.
