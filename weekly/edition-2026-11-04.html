Tesla

Seoul court dismisses Tesla owners' FSD refund lawsuit

Thursday, October 29, 2026 · Legal · Tesla

On October 29, 2026, the Seoul Central District Court ruled against Tesla owners who had sued Tesla Korea for refunds of the Full Self-Driving option, which Tesla has sold in South Korea since March 2017. The court found the option did not promise fully driverless (Level 5) driving, that Tesla had delivered Autopilot and Enhanced Autopilot functions and, through a recent update, traffic light and sign recognition and city driving on HW3 cars, and that Tesla was not liable for delays caused by technology limits or regulation. Reuters put the plaintiffs at 88; Maeil Business Newspaper reported 89, after nine of the original filers dropped out of the suit filed in December 2024.

RDW approves 'Tesla Assisted Driving' name and +10% speed cap in Europe

Tuesday, November 3, 2026 · Autonomy · Tesla

The Dutch vehicle authority RDW approved changes to FSD (Supervised) in Europe, which is renamed Tesla Assisted Driving on HW4 and later cars. The maximum speed margin drops from +50% to +10% and resets to 0% at each start; Tesla delivers the changes over the air. The existing type approval stays valid, and EU-wide approval is still pending in the TCMV.

Tesla opens Berlin Engineering Center in Köpenick

Wednesday, November 4, 2026 · Factory · Tesla

On November 4, 2026 Tesla opened the Berlin Tesla Engineering Center (BTEC) in Berlin-Köpenick, renovating an old factory complex of about 20,000 square metres between central Berlin and Giga Berlin. Tesla said the campus is its third such hub after Fremont and Shanghai, combining materials, powertrain, vehicle engineering, validation and safety testing in about 30 specialized laboratories and initially staffing about 250 engineers and technicians relocated from prior sites. The company said it is investing about €100 million and has applied for a second phase that could raise headcount to about 500.

xAI

Court denies xAI motion to dismiss NAACP Clean Air Act suit over Southaven turbines

Friday, October 30, 2026 · Legal · xAI · SpaceX

On October 30, 2026 Chief U.S. District Judge Debra M. Brown of the Northern District of Mississippi denied xAI's motion to dismiss the NAACP's Clean Air Act lawsuit over the gas turbines powering its Colossus 2 data center from Southaven, Mississippi. The court held that the turbines are not exempt mobile sources and need air permits, that the NAACP has standing, and that the citizen-suit provision is constitutional. The case proceeds; the court has not ruled on the NAACP's request for a preliminary injunction.

X

UK High Court rejects X, Meta and TikTok challenge to Ofcom data demands

Friday, October 30, 2026 · Legal · X

On October 30, 2026, London's High Court dismissed a challenge by X, Meta and TikTok to information notices Ofcom issued in February 2026 under the Online Safety Act, which sought detailed content-moderation metrics such as posts removed or restricted and users exposed to harmful content. X had called it the most burdensome information request it had received from any regulator, costing it millions of pounds; the court rejected the argument that the requests were disproportionate. It was one of the first court tests of the UK's online safety regime.
