Italy approves Tesla Assisted Driving Open in the timeline

Fri, Nov 20RegulatoryTesla

On November 20, 2026 Italy's Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport granted provisional type approval to Tesla Assisted Driving, the supervised FSD renamed for Europe, after a technical feasibility check. Transport Minister Matteo Salvini announced it on X, and Tesla Europe posted "Now approved in Italy, rollout begins soon" without a date. Italy is the 12th EU country to allow the system and the first of the bloc's large markets; an EU-wide vote is not expected before December.

SpaceX forgoes BEAD subsidies under new NTIA model agreement Open in the timeline

Mon, Nov 23RegulatorySpaceX

On November 23, 2026, NTIA announced a BEAD model agreement for low-Earth-orbit providers that keep their awarded locations but decline the subsidy, and SpaceX agreed to serve its Starlink locations under it. SpaceX must still deliver 100/20 Mbps, pass BEAD performance testing, give new subscribers equipment at no cost, offer a low-cost plan and report every six months. States can sign the model agreement or rebid the locations.

Boring Company signs 40-year Nashville airport station deal Open in the timeline

Thu, Nov 19InfrastructureBoring Co.Pending · not a promise

On November 19, 2026 The Boring Company said it had signed a 40-year agreement with the Metropolitan Nashville Airport Authority for a Music City Loop station at Nashville International Airport. The company says the station will sit 22 feet from the terminal entrance, with rides to downtown in under 10 minutes. The airport board had reviewed the license terms in February, with Boring paying to build, operate and maintain the system.
