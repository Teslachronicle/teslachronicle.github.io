FCC authorizes SpaceX's Starmind constellation of up to 1 million orbital data-center satellites Open in the timeline

Wed, Nov 25RegulatorySpaceXPending · not a promise

On November 25, 2026 the FCC Space Bureau granted in part SpaceX's January 2026 application for the SpaceX Orbital Data Center System (Starmind), a constellation of up to 1,000,000 satellites meant to operate as data centers in orbit at roughly 500 to 1,002 km (order DA 26-1273). The grant covers backup and emergency telemetry, tracking and command in Ka-band frequencies on a non-interference basis, and requires the first satellite to be operating by November 25, 2033, 10% of the constellation by 2035, 50% by 2038 and the full system by 2040. The FCC deferred SpaceX's request to dispose of satellites above 600 km without atmospheric re-entry; SpaceX must seek a modification before deploying any such satellites.
