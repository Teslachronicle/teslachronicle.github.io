NHTSA Special Order on Cybercab FMVSS certification (AQ26002) Open in the timeline

Thu, Sep 10LegalTeslaPending · not a promise

Dated September 10, 2026, NHTSA issued a Special Order in AQ26002 requiring Tesla to provide sworn answers by September 30, 2026 on the process and technical data behind Cybercab’s FMVSS self-certification after commercial service began September 3.
