Tesla launches Semi high-volume production at Nevada factory Open in the timeline

The volume truck uses in-house 4680 cells and a redesigned drivetrain; customers shown included PepsiCo, DHL, and US Foods.

Thu, Sep 24FactoryTeslaDelivers: Semi volume production planned…

At a September 24, 2026 (6pm PT) Semi Rollout event in Sparks, Nevada, Tesla Semi stated it was launching high-volume production at its first factory built for Semi—about 1.8 million sq ft, 50,000 units/year design capacity, all variants on one line—and that deliveries start now, including Tesla hauling its own freight. Reuters reported Tesla did not disclose current rate or pricing at the event.

Tesla signs $30B in new unsecured credit facilities, ends $5B 2023 revolver Open in the timeline

Tue, Sep 29FinanceTesla

On September 29, 2026, Tesla entered three senior unsecured credit agreements: a $20.0 billion three-year delayed-draw term loan (Citibank as agent; drawable up to ten times over 18 months, commitments stepping down to $10.0 billion after one year and $5.0 billion at 15 months; loans mature September 29, 2029), an $8.0 billion five-year revolver (Wells Fargo as agent; matures September 29, 2031; up to $500 million in letters of credit), and a $2.0 billion 364-day revolver (Wells Fargo; matures September 28, 2027). Revolver commitments can grow by up to $4.0 billion to $14.0 billion. Proceeds are for general corporate purposes, and no loans were outstanding at signing. The same day Tesla terminated its $5.0 billion January 20, 2023 revolving credit agreement, which had no borrowings and no early-termination penalty.
