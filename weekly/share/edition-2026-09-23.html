Tesla enables in-car Grok Bot with Connectors for hands-free tasks Open in the timeline

Tue, Sep 22ProductTesla

ZET SCALE alliance selects Tesla as primary OEM for 2,500 Class 8 BEVs Open in the timeline

Tue, Sep 22ProductTeslaWaiting

On September 22, 2026, Catalyst Mobility and Smart Freight Centre announced that the ZET SCALE shipper-carrier alliance enabled an order for 2,500 battery-electric Class 8 trucks—the largest U.S. electric Class 8 order to date—and selected Tesla as the primary OEM after a competitive RFP, with Kenworth, RIDE, and Volvo as secondary options. Founding shippers include Microsoft and PepsiCo; ZET Financial issues the purchase order and leases trucks. Tesla Semi Director Dan Priestley is quoted on the selection.
