Next-generation Roadster unveiled at SpaceX McGregor; reservations open Open in the timeline

The demo car hung upside down on a ramp using a fan suction system and flew, flipped and landed on cold-gas thrusters.

Thu, Oct 15ProductTesla

On the evening of October 15, 2026, Tesla unveiled the next-generation Roadster in a livestreamed event at SpaceX's test site in McGregor, Texas. Tesla opened reservations for three versions: Performance (road ready, $50,000 reservation payment), Thrust (rear thrusters, 0-60 mph in under one second, $250,000 reservation payment) and an invite-only, single-seat Rocket version not approved for public roads; $5,000 is due at booking and the balance within 10 days. Vehicle engineering chief Lars Moravy gave a top speed over 230 mph and range over 300 miles, and Musk said production starts in 2028. No purchase price or delivery date was given; Tesla's reservation agreement says the price is set at configuration and that Tesla cannot guarantee when or whether a car will be delivered, with reservations fully refundable until configuration.

Ninth Circuit throws out FSD false-advertising class certification Open in the timeline

Fri, Oct 16LegalTesla

On October 16, 2026, a Ninth Circuit panel (Judges Gould, Bybee and Bress) vacated the orders that let California buyers sue Tesla as classes over claims that cars sold since October 2016 had the hardware for full self-driving. The panel held that too many individual questions about what each buyer saw kept the claims from proceeding as a class action. The ruling does not decide the merits, and the underlying false-advertising case before U.S. District Judge Rita Lin remains pending.

Q3 2026 results: $28.0B revenue, $787M GAAP net income, $1.5B free cash flow Open in the timeline

Wed, Oct 21FinanceTesla

On October 21, 2026 Tesla furnished its Q3 2026 Update via Form 8-K Item 2.02 and held an audio-only Q&A webcast, streamed on X (63:47 replay). Unaudited Q3 figures: total revenue $28,046M; automotive $20,560M; energy generation and storage $2,881M; services and other $4,605M. GAAP operating income $718M; GAAP net income attributable to common stockholders $787M; diluted GAAP EPS $0.22; non-GAAP net income $1,697M ($0.48 per diluted share). Operating cash flow $6,263M; capex $4,732M; free cash flow $1,531M; cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments $45,447M. Operating cash flow included a $3,991M increase in accounts payable, accrued and other liabilities and a $1,255M increase in deferred revenue. Automotive gross margin excluding regulatory credits was 18.1% (16.3% in Q2). Energy gross margin rose to 33.1% from 20.4%, which CFO Vaibhav Taneja attributed mainly to tariff refunds recognized in the quarter and warranty true-downs agreed with a vendor. Active FSD subscriptions were 1.74M, excluding free trials. On the call Taneja said over 75% of North American deliveries included a paid FSD subscription sign-up at delivery, and added that every delivered car comes with a free month of FSD. The update says Optimus production began on Fremont's high-volume lines, volume production of Tesla Semi began, Megafactory Texas began production, and Tesla produced and delivered its 10 millionth vehicle.
