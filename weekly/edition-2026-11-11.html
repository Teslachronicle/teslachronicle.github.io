Musk: Cybertruck production at Giga Texas winds down mid-2027 for Cybercab

In a November 26, 2026 email, Tesla Taiwan told Cybertruck reservation holders that production would gradually end and their reservations would be cancelled automatically, with the NT$3,000 deposit returned and eight years of Premium Connectivity offered to those who order a Model 3 or Model Y and take delivery by March 31, 2027 (TeslaNorth and Drive Tesla Canada, citing News Pie and Electrify.tw).

Thursday, November 5, 2026 · Product · Tesla

On the evening of November 5, 2026 PT (06:30 UTC November 6), Elon Musk posted on X that Tesla will wind down Cybertruck production at Gigafactory Texas around the middle of next year to make room for increased Cybercab production. Cybertruck lead engineer Wes Morrill wrote that Cybertruck was a technology development platform that lives on through Cybercab; Musk later replied that the Cybertruck fleet will be around for a long time. Tesla did not say whether Cybertruck would move to another factory. A similar mainland China cancellation notice was shared on X but is not independently confirmed.

SpaceX seeks tax abatement for $24 billion Starbase gas plant and supercompute site

Monday, November 9, 2026 · Factory · SpaceX · xAI

A public hearing notice reported by Reuters on November 9, 2026 shows SpaceX plans a roughly $24 billion gas-fired power plant and super-compute facility at Starbase, Texas, and has applied for a property tax abatement. The Starbase City Commission will consider the abatement and an economic development agreement on December 9, 2026. In a November 12, 2026 official update, SpaceX said the project has already generated more than 3,000 construction jobs and is expected to support more than 500 permanent jobs once operational, will generate its own electricity without drawing power from the public grid, and has committed $220 million to expand Brownsville water and wastewater infrastructure, of which it said it has already provided $40 million to the Brownsville Public Utilities Board.

announced pending — Plan and abatement application only; commission vote set for December 9, 2026. The November 12, 2026 SpaceX update adds official figures on jobs (more than 3,000 construction already; more than 500 permanent once operational), off-grid self-generation, and a $220 million Brownsville water and wastewater commitment, of which SpaceX said $40 million has already been provided to the Brownsville Public Utilities Board. The $220 million city memorandum of understanding itself was approved earlier, on August 29, 2026; this row's date remains the November 9 abatement-notice report. On November 16, 2026 TCEQ issued the air permit for the 35-turbine, ~750 MW plant; The abatement vote is still pending. on November 17, 2026 Cameron County commissioners voted 3-2 against advancing a public hearing on SpaceX's proposed county tax agreement for the project (KRGV, via TeslaNorth, https://teslanorth.com/2026/11/18/spacex-cameron-county-tax-deal-response/). On November 18 SpaceX posted on X that the proposed payments in lieu of taxes were meant to give the county steadier revenue as computing equipment depreciates, and that it looks forward to further talks (https://x.com/SpaceX/status/2123193048574419212). The Starbase city process is separate; the project was not cancelled.

Tesla

Tesla opens Robotaxi service in San Antonio

Monday, November 9, 2026 · Autonomy · Tesla

On November 9, 2026 Tesla began Robotaxi rides in San Antonio, Texas, at 5 a.m. local time, after its Robotaxi X account announced the start just after midnight with a map of the launch geofence. The posted zone runs from Loop 1604 on the north side to just south of downtown, about 274 square miles by Robotaxi Tracker's measurement, though the airport inside it is not served. San Antonio is Tesla's eighth Robotaxi city; Tesla gave no fleet size, hours or safety-monitor details.

Site work begins at Tesla's $10.1 billion Project Crystal Sun solar factory site near Houston

Monday, November 9, 2026 · Factory · Tesla

Drone footage recorded November 8 and posted November 9, 2026 showed clearing, grading and equipment staging on Tesla's roughly 3,000-acre Project Crystal Sun site in Fort Bend County, Texas, and Musk replied "Project Crystal Sun" on X the same day. Fort Bend County approved incentive agreements for the project in October 2026.

SEC finishes paying out the 2018 "funding secured" Fair Fund

Tuesday, November 10, 2026 · Legal · Tesla

On November 10, 2026 the SEC told a Manhattan federal court it had finished distributing the $40 million Fair Fund built from the $20 million fines Musk and Tesla each paid to settle the 2018 "funding secured" case, and asked the judge to close it. A claims administrator paid $41.53 million, with interest and after fees, to 3,350 eligible claimants, covering 51.7% of their approved losses; 3,046 claims were rejected. About $449,000 left over goes to the US Treasury.

Tesla Robotaxi begins running 24/7 in every city it serves

Wednesday, November 11, 2026 · Autonomy · Tesla

On the evening of November 11, 2026 Tesla's official Robotaxi account posted that the service now runs 24/7 in every city where it is available, ending the overnight shutdown. That covers its eight unsupervised markets: Austin, Dallas, Fort Worth, Houston and San Antonio in Texas, and Miami, Orlando and Tampa in Florida. Hours had grown in steps from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. in August to 6 a.m. to 2 a.m. in late October, and Tesla had said on its October earnings call it would unlock 24/7 operation before the end of 2026.

Finland approves Tesla Assisted Driving

Wednesday, November 11, 2026 · Regulatory · Tesla

On November 11, 2026 Finland's transport agency Traficom said Tesla's Assisted Driving system, the supervised FSD renamed for Europe, is permitted on Finnish roads. Traficom recognized the Dutch RDW's provisional type approval, so Tesla can offer it to Finnish customers at once. Finland joins the Netherlands, Estonia, Croatia and Belgium ahead of a still-pending EU-wide decision.

SpaceX

FAA proposes streamlined commercial launch and re-entry rules

Thursday, November 5, 2026 · Regulatory · SpaceX

On November 5, 2026 the U.S. Federal Aviation Administration proposed streamlining its rules for commercial space launches and re-entries, including clearer thresholds for when a flight abort is mandatory and revised rules for vehicle breakup and falling-debris risk to people on the ground. The FAA said it plans to overhaul its space licensing framework next year and is expanding its Office of Commercial Space Transportation with a team of aerospace engineers to speed approvals. Reuters reported the changes would make launch approvals easier for SpaceX and Blue Origin; the White House goal is at least 1,000 launches and re-entries a year by 2030.

announced pending — Proposed rule only; not final. Licensing overhaul planned for 2027.

Neuralink

Neuralink registers PRIME2 study adding chronic ischemic stroke

Tuesday, November 10, 2026 · Regulatory · Neuralink

Neuralink's PRIME2 early feasibility study (NCT07929792) was first posted on ClinicalTrials.gov on November 10, 2026 and is listed as recruiting. It plans to enroll about 10 adults with spinal cord injury, ALS or chronic ischemic stroke, who will use the N1 implant, placed by the R1 robot, to control a cursor or virtual keyboard. It is the first Neuralink study to include stroke patients.

announced pending — Registered and recruiting; estimated start November 2026, no implants reported yet.

The Boring Company

Boring Company starts digging Morgan's Inclusion Tunnel in San Antonio

Tuesday, November 10, 2026 · Product · The Boring Company

On November 10, 2026 The Boring Company began tunneling on Morgan's Inclusion Tunnel, a nearly mile-long accessible underground link on the Morgan's Inclusion Campus in San Antonio, which serves people with disabilities. The route connects Morgan's Hotel and Morgan's Wonderland with stops at Morgan's Wonder Care and the Multi-Assistance Center; the Prufrock-5 machine arrived on site October 14. An anonymous private donor is funding the project, and no opening date was given.

announced pending — Construction started; no opening date announced.
