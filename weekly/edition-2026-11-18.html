Lead

Starship Flight 15: first upper-stage tower catch; booster lost offshore

SpaceX said on X on November 19 that the booster performed a safe abort on its catch return and splashed down off Starbase, and that its teams found little debris on the Mexican beach and could not clearly link it to the flight (https://x.com/SpaceX/status/2123516375805379040).

Saturday, November 14, 2026 · Product · SpaceX

Starship Flight 15 lifted off from Starbase Pad 2 at 4:47 p.m. CT on November 13, 2026, reached orbit and deployed 26 Starlink V3 satellites. After 14 orbits and about 22.5 hours, Ship 42 returned to Starbase on November 14 and was caught by the tower arms, the first catch of a Starship upper stage. Super Heavy Booster 22 ended its boostback burn early on a low fuel mass alarm, aborted its catch, diverted to the Gulf and was destroyed by its flight termination system after the landing burn. Mexico's environmental agency Profepa said on November 17 it was collecting debris at Playa Bagdad and would ask SpaceX to take responsibility (TeslaNorth, November 19).

Tesla

Tesla opens invite-only Cybercab orders for personal use at $29,990

Friday, November 13, 2026 · Autonomy · Tesla

On November 13, 2026 Tesla posted on X that select customers are being invited to buy a Cybercab for personal use, priced at $29,990 plus a required $299-a-month Autonomy Subscription, with an estimated delivery of December 2026. Buyers must take delivery in Texas, and the car runs unsupervised only inside Tesla's Robotaxi service areas; the order agreement bars resale without Tesla's permission and lets Tesla repurchase the vehicle. Musk said the fee is too high and should be closer to about $115 a month, and that availability will widen as regulators approve.

announced pending — Orders opened only; no customer-owned Cybercab delivered as of 2026-11-16. There is no public order page, and Tesla has not said how invitations are chosen. Owners cannot add their Cybercab to the Robotaxi network. buyer screenshots reported by Teslarati show Cybercab requires a $299/month Autonomy Subscription and 'will not operate without' one; it bundles autonomous operation, connectivity, remote support, roadside help and tire and windshield service (https://www.teslarati.com/tesla-cybercabs-autonomy-subscription-covers-all-bases-for-a-self-driving-car/). Monday, November 16, 2026 · Factory · Tesla

Tesla Energy unveils Powercore, a solid-state medium-voltage transformer

Tuesday, November 17, 2026 · Energy · Tesla

Tesla introduced Powercore on November 17, 2026 through its Tesla Megapack account on X: a single solid-state unit that combines a medium-voltage transformer, inverter and breaker, rated 7.4 MW (9 MVA) continuously at 34.5 kV AC with 10 MVA of grid-forming peak power. Tesla's spec sheet lists 800 to 2,000 V DC input, 98.86% peak efficiency and up to a 25-year warranty, and pitches it for Megablock storage, utility solar and 800 V DC data centers, where Tesla says it is working with Nvidia. It is made in the US, with first units operating in 2027; Tesla named no price, customer or factory.

announced pending — Announced only. First units are slated to operate in 2027; no price, customer or factory location has been disclosed. Fills in the transformer Tesla first described with Megablock in September 2025.

Tesla files for a $3.1 billion solar polysilicon refinery in Nueces County, Texas

Wednesday, November 18, 2026 · Factory · Tesla

A Texas JETI tax-incentive application posted November 18, 2026 shows Tesla proposing a $3.1 billion refinery near Robstown in Nueces County that would turn trichlorosilane into solar-grade polysilicon for high-efficiency solar cells. The filing targets construction from 2027, commercial operations in 2030, and 515 direct jobs by 2032. It would supply the raw material upstream of Tesla's Project Crystal Sun cell and panel factory.

announced pending — Application only, not an executed JETI agreement. Tesla says it is still comparing Texas with other sites and will build there only if it secures the tax deal. Jobs and spending are filing projections.

Texas issues air permit for SpaceX's Starbase gas plant and supercompute site

Monday, November 16, 2026 · Regulatory · SpaceX · xAI

On November 16, 2026 the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality issued an air permit for SpaceX's planned $24 billion supercompute site and gas-fired power plant near Starbase. The permit covers 35 natural-gas turbines totaling about 750 MW. TCEQ said it had resumed permitting of supercomputing facilities after Governor Greg Abbott ordered a pause in September; the permit came three days after environmental groups filed a complaint alleging construction had begun without one.

xAI

About 5,000 gallons of heat-transfer fluid spill at xAI's Southaven data center site

Saturday, November 14, 2026 · Regulatory · xAI · SpaceX

On November 14, 2026 about 5,000 gallons of DOWFROST HD, a propylene glycol-based heat-transfer fluid, spilled at xAI's facility in Southaven, Mississippi, and some reached a drainage ditch. SpaceX said a contractor had attached a misconfigured hose to a mobile tank; the Mississippi Department of Environmental Quality estimated 5,000 to 6,000 gallons. MDEQ said the fluid did not reach Horn Lake Creek, posed no immediate threat to public health and is overseeing cleanup.

X suspends AI training on Grok Bot third-party data at Irish regulator's request

Monday, November 16, 2026 · Regulatory · X · xAI

On November 16, 2026 Ireland's Data Protection Commission said X had confirmed it suspended using third-party personal data collected by its Grok Bot agent to train AI models. X had begun that processing in late October 2026; the DPC had raised significant concerns and asked for the suspension. The DPC said it would engage further with X on what happened and next steps.
