Lead

SpaceX commits to a $40B GPU finance lease with Apollo

Apollo and Goldman Sachs led the financing, with anchor commitments from BlackRock, Goldman Sachs Asset Management and KKR-advised accounts.

Monday, October 26, 2026 · Finance · SpaceX · xAI

On October 26, 2026 SpaceX announced a binding commitment, signed October 24, to enter an approximately $40 billion master finance lease with an entity managed by Apollo Global Management for GPU pods and related data-center equipment, including NVIDIA Vera Rubin chips for its Super Intelligence (Grok) compute buildout. The lease is expected to close before year-end 2026, with roughly five-year lease schedules starting as GPUs are delivered in the first half of 2027.

announced pending — Elon Musk posted on X on October 25, 2026 that SpaceX had closed the $40B GPU financing; the 8-K describes a binding commitment letter, with the master lease itself subject to closing conditions and expected before year-end 2026. Musk said it would not be a major factor in Q4 2026. Watch for the lease closing. Reported, not yet confirmed by SpaceX: Bloomberg said on November 17, 2026 that SpaceX could begin selling about $4 billion of bonds that week as part of the $40B financing, underwritten by Goldman Sachs and Apollo, with BlackRock- and KKR-linked funds also committing. Reported 2026-11-20, not confirmed by SpaceX: the Financial Times said investors bought more than $30 billion of debt tied to the lease on November 19, including a $25 billion investment-grade bond, and that some lenders sought a clause barring the pledged chips from being launched to orbit (via TeslaNorth, https://teslanorth.com/2026/11/20/spacex-lenders-ai-chips-orbit-ban/). Lease closing still pending.

Latvia approves FSD (Supervised), the tenth EU country

Monday, October 26, 2026 · Autonomy · Tesla

On October 26, 2026 Latvia's Road Traffic Safety Directorate (CSDD) recognized the Dutch RDW provisional EU type approval for Tesla's Level 2 driver-assistance system, sold in Europe as Tesla Assisted Driving (formerly FSD (Supervised)). CSDD said its decision weighed Tesla changes that lower the maximum speed-limit offset, reset any manual offset after each trip and strengthen driver monitoring. Tesla's Europe account posted that rollout begins soon; Reuters reported Latvia is the tenth EU country to allow the system.

Tesla expands Houston Robotaxi service into much of the 610 Loop

Monday, October 26, 2026 · Autonomy · Tesla

On October 26, 2026 Tesla widened its Houston Robotaxi service area from about 25 square miles around Jersey Village to roughly 251 to 254 square miles, announcing it on its Robotaxi X account with the post "Houston, we have liftoff." The new zone reaches downtown, Midtown, Montrose, the Heights, East Downtown and Bellaire, but leaves out part of the northeast Loop and both major airports. Rides run with no driver or front-seat safety monitor.

SpaceX

Anthropic's S-1 shows up to $120.5B in compute spending with SpaceXAI

Monday, October 26, 2026 · Finance · SpaceX · xAI

Anthropic's Form S-1, filed October 26, 2026, says it agreed to spend up to $120.5 billion with SpaceX.AI LLC, the SpaceX subsidiary formerly X.AI LLC, for NVIDIA GPU compute over terms running into 2029. Payments reach about $3.1 billion a month once all capacity is delivered, which Anthropic expects in December 2026. The agreements were signed and amended between May and October 2026, and after non-cancelable periods ending in January and March 2027 either side may terminate on 90 days' notice, so the total is a ceiling.

Air Force recovers Rocket Cargo pods from SpaceX Starfall capsule

Monday, October 26, 2026 · Product · SpaceX

A SpaceX Starfall reentry capsule launched on the Bandwagon-5 Falcon 9 rideshare from Cape Canaveral on October 20, 2026, carrying Air Force Research Laboratory cargo pods for a Rocket Cargo point-to-point delivery test. On October 26 AFRL posted that, with SpaceX's support, the pods were recovered after splashdown in the Pacific Ocean. The pods held sensors and sample gear such as a satellite tracker, batteries, a desalinator and medical equipment to measure temperature, shock and vibration through launch and reentry.

Neuralink

Neuralink reports first speech-restoration data: 75 words a minute in week one

Wednesday, October 28, 2026 · Product · Neuralink

On October 28, 2026, Neuralink released early data from its VOICE speech-restoration study, presented by principal investigator Dr. Nader Pouratian at the 2026 Annual NEALS Meeting. Neuralink said participants with ALS were producing text and voice at conversational rates, and that one participant reached 75 words per minute at a 5.8% word error rate on a 125,000-word vocabulary in their first week. The N1 device remains investigational and is not FDA-approved.
