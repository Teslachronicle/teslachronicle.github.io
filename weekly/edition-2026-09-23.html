Tesla enables in-car Grok Bot with Connectors for hands-free tasks

Tuesday, September 22, 2026 · Product · Tesla

On September 22, 2026, Tesla posted that Grok in the car can now do meaningful work through Connectors, such as managing an inbox, cleaning up a calendar, or talking through existing files, chats and tasks hands-free. Not a Tesla App reported the feature as Grok Bot in Tesla vehicles, available on SuperGrok tiers with recent vehicle software and separate from the earlier in-car Grok chat.

ZET SCALE alliance selects Tesla as primary OEM for 2,500 Class 8 BEVs

Tuesday, September 22, 2026 · Product · Tesla

On September 22, 2026, Catalyst Mobility and Smart Freight Centre announced that the ZET SCALE shipper-carrier alliance enabled an order for 2,500 battery-electric Class 8 trucks—the largest U.S. electric Class 8 order to date—and selected Tesla as the primary OEM after a competitive RFP, with Kenworth, RIDE, and Volvo as secondary options. Founding shippers include Microsoft and PepsiCo; ZET Financial issues the purchase order and leases trucks. Tesla Semi Director Dan Priestley is quoted on the selection.
