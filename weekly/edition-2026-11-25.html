Tesla files with German cartel office to buy assets of machine-tool maker EMAG

Monday, November 23, 2026 · Factory · Tesla

On November 23, 2026 Tesla notified Germany's Federal Cartel Office (Bundeskartellamt) of an asset acquisition from the EMAG Group, a machine-tool maker based in Salach near Stuttgart; the office published the filing on November 27 as case B5-132/26. The notice covers manufacturing systems and machines for precision and electrochemical machining of metal parts and laser processing. Tesla had earlier set up a renamed company, Tesla Machining Technologies Management SE, at EMAG's headquarters; no price or scope has been disclosed.

announced pending — Merger-control review pending; Tesla and EMAG have not commented publicly. Press links the deal to Optimus actuator and gear production; the filing itself does not say where Tesla would use the equipment. Roughly 220 staff moving over is press-reported only.

SpaceX forgoes BEAD subsidies under new NTIA model agreement

Monday, November 23, 2026 · Regulatory · SpaceX

On November 23, 2026, NTIA announced a BEAD model agreement for low-Earth-orbit providers that keep their awarded locations but decline the subsidy, and SpaceX agreed to serve its Starlink locations under it. SpaceX must still deliver 100/20 Mbps, pass BEAD performance testing, give new subscribers equipment at no cost, offer a low-cost plan and report every six months. States can sign the model agreement or rebid the locations.

FCC authorizes SpaceX's Starmind constellation of up to 1 million orbital data-center satellites

Wednesday, November 25, 2026 · Regulatory · SpaceX

On November 25, 2026 the FCC Space Bureau granted in part SpaceX's January 2026 application for the SpaceX Orbital Data Center System (Starmind), a constellation of up to 1,000,000 satellites meant to operate as data centers in orbit at roughly 500 to 1,002 km (order DA 26-1273). The grant covers backup and emergency telemetry, tracking and command in Ka-band frequencies on a non-interference basis, and requires the first satellite to be operating by November 25, 2033, 10% of the constellation by 2035, 50% by 2038 and the full system by 2040. The FCC deferred SpaceX's request to dispose of satellites above 600 km without atmospheric re-entry; SpaceX must seek a modification before deploying any such satellites.

announced pending — License granted in part and deferred in part; no Starmind satellites launched. The authorization covers radio links for backup TT&C only (primary links are optical) and does not establish that orbital data centers are technically or economically viable (Ars Technica). The non-re-entry disposal waiver for satellites above 600 km is deferred.

SpaceX commits one new share to every active Trump Account; Musk to match

Wednesday, November 25, 2026 · Finance · SpaceX

On November 25, 2026 SpaceX announced it will contribute one newly issued share of Class A common stock to every currently active Trump Account, the tax-advantaged investment accounts for American children. Elon Musk will personally match the number of shares SpaceX contributes, so each account would get two shares, and President and COO Gwynne Shotwell separately announced a personal gift of over 2 million SpaceX shares. SpaceX's release says the contributions are subject to risks including third-party and government approvals.

announced pending — Commitment only; no deposit date given. The number of eligible accounts and total shares are not stated by SpaceX. Not a Tesla App estimated about 72 million shares from each side (roughly 0.5% dilution from SpaceX's newly issued half) using a count of US children under 18, which may differ from the number of active Trump Accounts. On November 26 Musk wrote on X, 'I will be donating another share of SpaceX to all kids in America every year' (personal pledge; no mechanism stated). He said SpaceX's share transfers next month and his own matching share when his lockup expires in June 2027 (Teslarati).

Neuralink

Neuralink receives FDA approval to launch LINK pivotal study

Wednesday, November 25, 2026 · Regulatory · Neuralink

Neuralink announced on November 25, 2026 that the FDA approved the LINK Pivotal Study, the first pivotal trial of an implantable brain-computer interface in the US. It will test the safety and effectiveness of the N1 system in adults with severe paralysis from ALS. Neuralink says it has 30+ implanted participants across early feasibility studies in the US, Canada and the UK. The approval is to run the trial; the implant is not approved for commercial use.

announced pending — FDA approval announced; enrollment open for people with ALS, no pivotal-study implants reported yet.
